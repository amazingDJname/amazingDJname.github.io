Notation

The accidentals in JI-ify belong to the Helmholtz-Ellis JI Pitch Notation (HEJI), devised by Marc Sabat and Wolfgang von Schweinitz and revised in 2020 by Marc Sabat and Thomas Nicholson. Its legend is published by Plainsound Music Edition.

Music glyphs

Staves, clefs, noteheads and accidentals are drawn with JIify Notation, a subset of 61 glyphs taken from Bravura 1.482, the music font by Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH. Bravura is released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which asks that a modified version carry a different name; the subset is distributed under the same license. Copyright notice and license
